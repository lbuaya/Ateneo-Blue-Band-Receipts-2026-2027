Encoding Guide

Receipt Encoding Tutorial

Learn how to use the Ateneo Blue Band Receipt Encoder to record customer purchases, manage items, and print official receipts.

🎫 Open Generator • ⚙️ Google Sheets Setup Guide • 🔍 Verify Receipts

Steps 1 – 3  ·  Setup & Input
1
Verify Google Sheets URL Connection
Open the Receipt Encoder page in your browser. Scroll to the Google Sheets Setup section and verify that your Google Apps Script Web App URL is pasted in the input field. This ensures that transactions are sent to your spreadsheet automatically.
Need the URL? If you haven't connected a sheet yet, follow the Google Sheets Setup Guide to generate and paste one. Once pasted, the URL is saved in your browser's local memory.
Ensure the Web App URL is configured in the Google Sheets Setup section
2
Add Preset Items (Optional)
To speed up encoding, configure common band items (such as band shirts, tickets, or merchandise) under the Preset Items (Optional) section. Click + Add Preset Item, fill in the name and price, and the items will save locally. They will now appear in a convenient dropdown inside each item row.
Local Storage: Presets are saved in your browser's local storage, meaning you won't need to retype them when you open the page next time.
3
Enter Transaction & Customer Details
Fill in the customer fields under the form: **Name**, **Grade and Section**, and **Email**. Then, select the **Payment Type** (Cash, GCash, or Bank Transfer). Generate a secure, sequential invoice number by clicking **Generate Invoice No.** (e.g., `INV-20260531-0008`), or enter a custom identifier.
Steps 4 – 7  ·  Items, submission & print
4
Add Items, Quantities, and Pricing
Enter the purchased items in the items area. You can click **+ Add Item** to insert new rows. If you have configured preset items, choose one from the dropdown to auto-populate the Description and Price, or type them in manually. Enter the correct **Qty** and **Price**; the row amount, subtotal, and total will instantly recalculate.
Removing Items: If a row was added by mistake or needs to be removed, click the red × button next to that row.
5
Submit Transaction to Google Sheets
Once all fields are verified, click the dark blue Done (Fill & Send to Sheets) button. The system will compile the transaction data, draw the barcode at the top, render the receipt details, and send the data payload to your connected Google Sheet. A green success message will confirm: "Successfully sent to Google Sheets!".
6
Print the Receipt with Correct Layout Settings
After clicking Done, the browser's system print dialog will open automatically (or click the **Print Receipt** button manually). To ensure the receipt is sized correctly and contains all graphic visual designs, configure your settings exactly as follows:

1. **Destination**: Select Save as PDF (to save digitally) or choose a physical printer.
2. **Margins**: Set to None. This eliminates extra padding and fits the design perfectly.
3. **Options (Background Graphics)**: Check the box next to Background graphics. This renders the blue header, borders, and signature graphics.
PDF Filename Tip: The generator dynamically changes the page title to the invoice sequence number (e.g. 8) during printing. When you save as PDF, the filename will automatically default to that number, saving you manual naming steps.
Optimal layout: Margins set to None · Background graphics checkbox enabled
7
Reset the Encoder for the Next Customer
After the receipt has been successfully printed or saved, click the red Clear All Fields button. This wipes all customer details, clears the receipt preview pane, and resets the items list to a single blank row, preparing the system for the next buyer.
✅ Ready to go! Keep this guide handy during encoding sessions. Using pre-defined presets makes transaction encoding fast, clean, and error-free.
